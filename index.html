01 / Research

From geometric structure
to mathematical insight.

My interests lie at the intersection of geometry, variational methods, and computation.

01

Discrete differential
geometry

Convex polyhedral surfaces, discrete curvature, and variational approaches to geometric comparison and rigidity.

Convex caps · Discrete Schwarz lemmas

02

Hyperbolic
geometry

Polyhedral geometry in negatively curved spaces, with an emphasis on the relationship between geometric structures and analytic methods.

Polyhedral geometry · Variational methods

03

Geometric

Algorithmic questions inspired by non-Euclidean geometry, including graph constructions, navigation, and geometric optimization.

Graphs · Optimization · Computation
