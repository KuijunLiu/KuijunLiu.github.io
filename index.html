Research

The Liouville property for geometric graphs in the plane

With Feng Luo, Yanwen Luo, and Zhenghao Rao · arXiv:2610.39242

We prove that every bounded function harmonic for the cotangent Laplacian on a locally finite Delaunay triangulation of the plane is constant. The result also holds for nonnegative weights that connect all vertices and make affine functions harmonic. Cotangent-weighted Delaunay triangulations can be transient, but uniformly bounded edge lengths guarantee recurrence.

Discrete Schwarz lemmas for convex caps

With Feng Luo, Yanwen Luo, and Zhenghao Rao · In preparation

We establish discrete Schwarz lemmas that compare the heights of admissible convex caps with the same intrinsic roof using curvature and boundary data. The results cover generalized Euclidean caps and hyperbolic caps with hyperideal vertices, and give a discrete Schwarz–Pick lemma for circle patterns.

Navigable Graphs for Low-Dimensional Hyperbolic Spaces

With Chengyuan Deng, Jie Gao, Kevin Lu, Feng Luo, Puheng Weng, and Cheng Xin · To appear in ITCS 2027 · arXiv:2610.24874

For n points in hyperbolic space of fixed dimension d, we construct graphs that allow routing to any target by repeatedly moving closer to it. They use Od(n) edges, with constant maximum out-degree if Od(n) Steiner points are added. A refined construction uses Od(n log n) edges and supports a greedy-routing algorithm with O(log2 n) expected hops.
