Research

I study how geometric structure, from discrete curvature and convexity to the exponential volume growth of hyperbolic space, controls rigidity phenomena and the efficiency of algorithms.

Navigable Graphs for Low-Dimensional Hyperbolic Spaces

Submitted to ITCS 2027

A graph on a point set is navigable if greedy routing, which always moves to the neighbor closest to the target, reaches every target. We show that n-point sets in Hk admit navigable graphs with Ok(n) edges and O(log2 n) expected greedy hops, with matching lower bounds. Navigable graphs model the graph-based indexes, such as HNSW, behind large-scale nearest-neighbor search.

Discrete Schwarz Lemma for Polyhedral Surfaces

In preparation

Rigidity and comparison results for convex polyhedral caps in Euclidean and hyperbolic settings, giving discrete counterparts of the classical Schwarz lemma. The proofs combine variational principles, curvature maps, and Hessian analysis.

Projects

Cross-sectional return prediction

Personal project, 2026 · Code

Monthly prediction on 1.35 million CRSP stock-months (1994–2024) with 14 momentum, reversal, volatility, trend, size, and liquidity predictors and ridge regression tuned by expanding-window cross-validation. An audit of my own labels uncovered a look-ahead bias that had inflated out-of-sample IC from 0.015 to 0.080; once fixed, the signal proved weak and did not survive transaction costs.

Financial fraud detection

Team project, The Erdős Institute, 2026 · Code

Fraud detection on the Elliptic and Elliptic++ Bitcoin transaction graphs, with chronological splits to prevent temporal leakage. An XGBoost baseline reached 0.923 ROC-AUC and 0.793 PR-AUC on the temporal test set, benchmarked against graph neural networks.
